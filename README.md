# VerdantRochesteriGEM
Farmer-friendly estimates of when fall armyworm caterpillars are most likely eating corn leaves, based on local weather and planting date. Available in English and Spanish.

This tool estimates the dates when fall armyworm caterpillars are most likely feeding on corn leaves rather than the ear. Enter your city and planting date, and it shows when corn reaches each growth stage and when each caterpillar wave is likely to feed on leaves. It is a research prototype from the Rochester iGEM 2026 VERDANT team and has not been tested in real fields, so use it alongside your own scouting and your local extension office’s advice.
